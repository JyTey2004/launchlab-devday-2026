One experiment, from end to end.

The film follows a public Vite repository through a pinned version, GPT-assisted measurement plan, founder approval, isolated instrumentation, CodeBuild and Amplify deployment, then consent-based activity and voluntary feedback. A founder or authenticated calling agent can retrieve the results.

The snapshot contains two browser sessions, two recorded checkout actions (shown as 2+ because earlier repeats are unavailable), and one internal QA response. These verify collection; they do not establish customer demand.
